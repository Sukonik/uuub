What is Impasto?

Cocoa, worked into a ritual.

An Impasto is our proposed concentrated cocoa preparation: cocoa worked with a little hot water and sweetness to order into a smooth, rich base. It can stand alone or become the heart of a hot or iced drink. This is a product concept, not a finalized recipe.

Morning at UUUB

A new morning ritual, made from cocoa.

The morning worker spirit is central to UUUB: a thoughtful cocoa drink that fits the trip to work, a short pause before the day, or a conversation at the counter. We want the flavor bold, the sweetness adjustable, and the service quick enough for a real morning routine.

Cocoa naturally contains caffeine and theobromine, but UUUB does not claim a guaranteed "no crash," cardiovascular benefit, or breakfast replacement. Any future nutrition claim needs the finished recipe and appropriate review.

Mission and future house

The world's golden standard for cocoa.

Our mission: to become the world's golden standard for cocoa by discovering exceptional origins, perfecting the Impasto, and making a remarkable cup of cocoa a beloved daily ritual. A UUUB Cocoa House would bring together a specialty beverage bar, a chocolate house, and an origin-discovery space — COCOA → PASTE → IMPASTO → DRINK, made visible at the counter.
